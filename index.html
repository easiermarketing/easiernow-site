What is Easier Now?

Easier Now is a to-do list app that schedules your tasks into your working day and shows you 1 at a time, the most valuable first. 14 days free, no card.

How it decides

It knows what each task is worth to you.

Easier Now knows how long each task takes, what happens if it is late, and what an hour of it is worth. You set that in 3 taps, or type the task in plain English.

  • Urgency Red What goes wrong if this is late?
  • Importance Blue Does this get me somewhere I want to go?
  • Pay grade Green and yellow Should I be the one doing this?
  • Priority star Purple Is this the one thing, today?

Questions people ask before starting

I have abandoned every productivity app. Why would Easier Now be different?

Easier Now has nothing to maintain. There is no board to tidy, no weekly setup and no daily planning session. Miss a fortnight and you are handed the backlog one item at a time, with 3 buttons under each: skip it, do it today, or mark it done.

What if Easier Now does not work for me?

If this is your first subscription, ask us within 30 days of your first payment and we refund it. No form, and you do not have to tell us why.

What happens on day 15?

Your 14 days need no card, so nothing is charged when they end. On day 15 everything you put in is still there and you can still read all of it. What you cannot do is add or change things until you pick a plan. Which plan you need follows what you actually used: stay inside three projects and five routines and Starter covers you, go past that and it is Pro.

Is there an app for my phone?

Not yet. Easier Now is built for the desktop today, where the work gets done. It runs in your browser and installs to your dock or taskbar so it opens in its own window with its own icon. A phone version is being built and you can follow it on the roadmap.

How long does it take to set up?

About a minute. Easier Now asks your name, when you wake and sleep, your working hours, and whether to connect Google Calendar and send notifications. Routine templates are 1 tap each, so the things you repeat are in before you have finished reading this answer.
