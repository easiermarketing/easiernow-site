What is Easier Now?

Easier Now is a to-do list app that plans your working day automatically. You add tasks, it works out how long each one takes and what it is worth to you, then shows you one task at a time instead of a list. It is free for 14 days with no card, and your account stays readable after that.

The problem

Death by a thousand cuts is the productivity killer.

4 people asked you for something small. Each one took about 20 minutes. You did all 4, because they were quick and because somebody was waiting.

What changes

What changes, by about the second week.

Not the features. The difference you notice.

  • You sit down and start working, instead of deciding. The choosing already happened. Whatever is on screen is the task to do, so the first 20 minutes go into the work rather than into picking.
  • You stop carrying the list around. Nothing is waiting to be remembered, so there is no background hum of half-recalled jobs while you are trying to think about one of them.
  • The important task wins, not the loud one. You set once what urgency and importance are worth to you. Easier Now applies that at 4pm, when you no longer can.
  • The habits you keep meaning to build hold. Miss a routine and it is due again tomorrow instead of disappearing, so one bad week does not send you back to the start.
  • You finish the day knowing what you did. Every task is logged as you finish it, with no timer to start. You can put it down at 6pm and leave it down.

Catch-up mode

Falling behind? Catch-up mode works in seconds.

You were ill, or away, or the week overran. 71 tasks are waiting. Easier Now shows them one at a time: keep it, move it, delete it. Each takes a couple of seconds, and routines you missed are due again shortly anyway, so most take a single tap. 71 tasks clear in about 4 minutes.

Projects

Always know the answer to "when can you get that done by?"

Add a project and its tasks lay themselves across the weeks ahead, in your calendar, with real durations. When a client asks for Thursday the 14th, you can see whether Thursday the 14th is possible before you reply.

Teams

Hand it over and watch it move.

Give a task to someone and it leaves your list and joins theirs. The project page shows where everything has got to, so the Monday meeting is you reading a screen for 90 seconds instead of asking 5 people for updates.

How it decides

Why it works when the last app did not.

Most apps keep your list in the order you typed it. Easier Now knows how long each task takes, what happens if it is late, and what an hour of it is worth, so it can give you the task that should be next. Set that yourself in 3 taps, or type the task in plain English and let Easier Now set it.

The rest of a working week.

Four things you will meet in the first fortnight.

  • Routines Set a routine once. Daily, weekly, monthly or yearly. Miss one and it moves to the next day instead of becoming 10 copies of itself.
  • The Info tab Checkboxes in your notes become tasks. Write the actions while the call runs, press + Task, and confirm the cards before Easier Now makes them.

Questions people ask before starting

I have abandoned every productivity app. Why would Easier Now be different?

Easier Now has nothing to maintain. There is no board to tidy, no weekly setup and no daily planning session. Miss a fortnight and you are handed the backlog one item at a time, with three buttons under each: skip it, do it today, or mark it done.

What if Easier Now does not work for me?

If this is your first subscription, ask us within 30 days of your first payment and we refund it. No form, and you do not have to tell us why.

What happens on day 15?

Your 14 days need no card, so nothing is charged when they end. On day 15 everything you put in is still there and you can still read all of it. What you cannot do is add or change things until you pick a plan. Which plan you need follows what you actually used: stay inside three projects and five routines and Starter covers you, go past that and it is Pro.

Is there an app for my phone?

Not yet. Easier Now is built for the desktop today, where the work gets done. It runs in your browser and installs to your dock or taskbar so it opens in its own window with its own icon. A phone version is being built and you can follow it on the roadmap.

How long does it take to set up?

About a minute. Easier Now asks your name, when you wake and sleep, your working hours, and whether to connect Google Calendar and send notifications. Routine templates are one tap each, so the things you repeat are in before you have finished reading this answer.
